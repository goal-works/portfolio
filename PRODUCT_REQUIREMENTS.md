# Product Requirements

## Goal
Create a professional portfolio for Kazuki (James) that demonstrates senior engineering judgment and establishes a distinctive specialization in AI evaluation, agentic systems, and production software engineering.

## Primary Audience
- AI engineering teams
- AI evaluation / benchmark teams
- technically sophisticated startup founders
- engineering leaders
- collaborators seeking senior full-stack or AI engineering capability

## Success Criteria
A visitor should understand within the first screen:
1. who James is professionally,
2. that his current focus is AI evaluation and intelligent systems,
3. that he has broad software engineering experience,
4. where to see proof of his work.

Within two minutes, a technical reviewer should be able to identify:
- flagship projects
- architecture depth
- technology range
- current AI specialization
- approach to reliability/testing
- public GitHub path
- contact path

## Portfolio Evidence Model
Every completed flagship project should eventually provide:
- working application/demo
- public repository
- technical case study
- polished screenshots / architecture diagram

## Confidential Work
Private work should be described at a category/capability level. Do not expose proprietary source code, benchmark tasks, clients, company-private interfaces, or confidential data.

## Launch Threshold
The portfolio can launch when:
- site quality gate passes,
- EvalForge has a real public repository,
- EvalForge has a working demonstrable workflow,
- EvalForge has screenshots and a technical case study.

Other projects may remain clearly marked `In Development`.
