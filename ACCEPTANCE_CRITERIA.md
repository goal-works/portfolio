# Portfolio Acceptance Criteria

## Content
- Hero clearly communicates James's AI-focused specialization.
- No invented surname, employers, clients, education, dates, metrics, or outcomes.
- Private work is described professionally without proprietary disclosure.
- Incomplete public projects are labeled honestly.
- No lorem ipsum or low-quality placeholder copy remains in launchable sections.

## Navigation and Routes
- `/` works.
- `/work` works.
- `/about` works.
- Four flagship case-study routes work.
- 404 is branded and functional.
- header/footer links are correct.

## Design
- technical editorial aesthetic is consistent.
- typography hierarchy is strong.
- spacing is deliberate.
- project screenshots/placeholder states are consistently framed.
- no generic skill bars/progress rings.
- no excessive gradients/glassmorphism/particle effects.

## Responsive
- no unintended horizontal scrolling.
- 375px, 430px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px, and 1920px layouts are usable.
- mobile navigation is usable and accessible.
- large headings do not clip.

## Accessibility
- keyboard navigation works.
- focus is visible.
- reduced motion is respected.
- semantic headings are valid.
- image alt text is meaningful.
- contrast meets reasonable AA requirements.

## SEO
- title/description metadata exists.
- sitemap exists.
- robots metadata/file exists.
- Open Graph metadata exists.
- no false structured data.

## Engineering
- strict TypeScript where applicable.
- lint passes.
- typecheck passes.
- relevant tests pass.
- production build passes.
- no secrets committed.
- no unnecessary heavy dependencies introduced.

## Launch Readiness
Initial public launch is acceptable when the portfolio quality gates above pass and EvalForge is a genuine public engineering artifact with a usable workflow, documentation, screenshots, and case study.
